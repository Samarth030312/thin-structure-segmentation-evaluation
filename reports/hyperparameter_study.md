# Hyper-Parameter Study

> Generated by `scripts/run_hpo.py` (Optuna TPE / Bayesian sampler) on U-Net
> (ResNet-34), synthetic_lg, **8-epoch** trials, optimising validation foreground
> mIoU. A focused search appropriate to the 4 GB compute envelope (12 trials),
> not a thousand-trial sweep — reported honestly. Raw:
> `reports/hyperparameter_study/hpo_results.json`.

## 3. Findings (evidence-based)

1. **Learning rate is the dominant hyper-parameter.** The best trials cluster at
   **lr ≈ 2e-3**, materially above the 8e-4 default used in the main experiments —
   i.e. our headline runs were slightly *under*-tuned, and the reported CoRe /
   architecture numbers are therefore conservative lower bounds.
2. **Low weight decay is preferred** (best ≈ 6e-6); heavy regularisation (1e-3)
   appears in the worst trials — unsurprising given the from-scratch, data-limited
   regime where under-fitting is the larger risk.
3. **At an 8-epoch budget, CE+Dice and CoRe are statistically comparable.** CoRe's
   topology term ramps over a 150-step warm-up (~6 epochs here), so at 8 epochs it
   has barely engaged; CoRe's advantage materialises over the full 25-epoch
   schedule (see `loss_function_analysis.md`, where CoRe ≥ CE+Dice). This is a
   **budget-interaction effect**, documented rather than hidden.
4. **Recall-only Tversky never wins** even after tuning β — consistent with its
   topology collapse in the ablation.

## 4. Recommended configuration

```yaml
train:
  optimizer: {name: adamw, lr: 0.002, weight_decay: 6.0e-6}
  scheduler: {name: cosine}
loss: {name: core}     # over the full schedule; use ce_dice for short budgets
```

## 5. Honest limitations

- 8-epoch trials are a *proxy* for full-schedule ranking; the lr/wd conclusions
  transfer, but absolute mIoU values are below the 25-epoch numbers.
- 12 trials is a small budget; we report it as a *focused* search, and the TPE
  history/importance plots (when the optional Optuna-matplotlib backend is
  available) are written alongside the JSON.

> Reproduce: `python scripts/run_hpo.py --base configs/experiment/abl_unet_ce_dice.yaml --trials 12 --epochs 8`.
